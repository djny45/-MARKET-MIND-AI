# Historical distribution analysis

## Purpose
Describe the empirical distribution of a user-defined historical metric over a fixed sample.

## Procedure
1. Record the metric definition, sample boundaries, frequency, and source.
2. Report sample count, quantiles, dispersion, and missing observations.
3. Use out-of-sample splits when evaluating a research hypothesis.
4. Test sensitivity to the sample window and parameter assumptions.
5. State clearly that historical frequencies do not determine future outcomes.

## Boundaries
Do not produce entry/exit probabilities, price targets, timing advice, or trade instructions. No live market monitoring or brokerage integration.
