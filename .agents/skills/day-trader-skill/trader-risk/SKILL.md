# Research methodology audit

## Purpose
Evaluate the reliability of a historical analysis, not the financial risk of a person's portfolio.

## Procedure
1. Check train/test chronology and ensure features do not use future information.
2. Inspect survivorship, selection, look-ahead, and data-snooping risks.
3. Review sample size, missingness, corporate actions, and source consistency.
4. Check whether repeated parameter tuning invalidates the reported evaluation.
5. Return findings with severity, evidence, and a reproducible remediation.

## Boundaries
Do not approve orders, set investment risk limits, recommend leverage or short selling, or provide personal financial advice. No broker or order tools.
