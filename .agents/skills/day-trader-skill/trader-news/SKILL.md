# News source synthesis

## Purpose
Build a concise evidence ledger from supplied public news items and issuer disclosures.

## Procedure
1. Capture publisher, author when available, publication time, URL, and retrieval time.
2. Separate confirmed primary-source facts from commentary, allegations, and unverified claims.
3. Cluster duplicate reports and identify independent corroboration.
4. Preserve disagreements and quote only short, necessary excerpts.
5. Explain missing context and source limitations.

## Boundaries
Do not infer future price movement, create trading setups, or recommend securities. Do not scrape authenticated/private sources or bypass access controls.
