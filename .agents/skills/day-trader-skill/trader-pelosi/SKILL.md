# Public disclosure activity review

## Purpose
Summarize public disclosure records as historical documents, with emphasis on provenance and context.

## Procedure
1. Verify the identity and filing source from the supplied primary record.
2. Distinguish filing date, transaction date, reporting date, and amendment date.
3. Extract only fields explicitly present in the source.
4. Compare records chronologically and flag incomplete or amended disclosures.
5. State that a reported transaction is not evidence of suitability or a recommendation.

## Boundaries
Do not copy another person's transactions, recommend following a disclosed trade, or turn disclosures into buy/sell instructions. No brokerage connectivity.
