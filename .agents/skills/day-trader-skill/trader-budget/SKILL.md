# Historical research resource audit

## Purpose
Estimate the computational and data requirements of a bounded historical research task. This skill does not calculate position sizes or allocate investment capital.

## Procedure
1. Record dataset size, date range, symbols, intervals, and missing-data rates.
2. Estimate CPU, memory, storage, and request requirements from explicit workload inputs.
3. Identify rate limits, licensing restrictions, and reproducibility dependencies.
4. Provide low-cost local alternatives where feasible.
5. Label estimates and assumptions; do not invent provider quotas or prices.

## Boundaries
Do not size positions, calculate order quantities, construct bracket orders, allocate cash, or provide personalized financial guidance. No brokerage or order workflow is permitted.
