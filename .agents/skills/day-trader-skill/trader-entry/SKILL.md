# Historical pattern review

## Purpose
Describe patterns already present in supplied historical data and test whether their labels are reproducible.

## Procedure
1. Validate source provenance, interval, timezone, and chronological ordering.
2. Identify completed bars and the exact historical window used.
3. Describe indicator values and pattern labels as retrospective observations.
4. Include counterexamples, missing observations, and sensitivity to parameter choices.
5. Avoid extrapolating historical patterns into future outcomes.

## Boundaries
Do not provide entry points, buy/sell signals, alerts to enter positions, or order parameters. Do not connect to broker APIs or real-time execution services.
